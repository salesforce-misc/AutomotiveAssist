Key Roles and Responsibilities in Automotive Cloud

Learn about the different user personas in a typical Automotive Cloud implementation. Use standard
and custom permission sets to customize Automotive Cloud for your users’ needs.


**REQUIRED EDITIONS**


Available in: Enterprise, Unlimited, and Developer Editions.


Typical Roles in Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) Implementations

Role                               Responsibilities


## Dealer Performance Manager or

Dealer Principal                   Manages multiple dealers and tracks compliance and performance.


## Dealer principals assess key performance metrics (KPIs) such as

lead conversion rate, new vehicle sales, sales agreements
compliance, age of inventory, and revenue margins. Based on the
analysis, they can plan dealer visits, review incentive structures,
organize salesperson trainings, and revise targets.


## Chief Experience Officer

Improves dealer experience and increase customer loyalty.


## Chief experience officers work closely with marketing teams to

segment leads and create personalized journeys and offers, engage
dealers with relevant and timely information about vehicles, and
communicate critical recalls and service schedules.


## Customer Service Operations

Manager                            Provides timely support to customers and dealers.


## Service operations managers contextualize customer interactions

based on information gathered from the vehicle console and
service console. They can create action plans and follow up on
interactions, cases, and milestones.


## Territory Sales Manager

Leads the sales team and ensure that company goals are met.

<!-- page:16 -->




Role                           Responsibilities



## Territory sales managers, such as district sales managers, are

responsible for planning the list of sales KPIs that help in evaluating
dealer sales, conducting sales training, and setting up sales targets.
They’re also involved in vehicle and spares forecasting and
managing new programs for vehicles and spares.


## Technical Service Specialist

Provides timely support to customers and dealers on how to
effectively service and repair vehicles.

Technical service specialists analyze knowledge base information,
checks the service history of a vehicle, and telematics information
to plan repairs and scheduled maintenance. They’re also
responsible for providing information on compatible parts and
accessories for vehicles, and suggesting upgrades and
replacements.


## Service Representative

Manages communication with dealers and assist with product
knowledge, procedures, and sales queries.


## Service representatives manage incoming and outgoing

communication with dealer representatives. They can resolve
queries related to claims, vehicle deliveries, parts replenishment, or
product knowledge. They also upsell products and plan visits to
dealer locations to carry out audit tasks.


Typical Roles in Dealer Implementations

Role                                              Responsibilities


## Dealer Representative

Tracks sales, inventory, and pipeline for a specific
dealership.


## Dealer representatives have visibility into vehicle

inventory, leads, sales, service, and spare parts
sales. They follow up on leads, organize test drives,
book appointments for customers, and offer right
vehicles to the right customers at the right time.


## Dealer Service Executive

Offers in-depth advice on service and
maintenance strategies to maximize customer

<!-- page:17 -->




Role                            Responsibilities


retention and drive profitability.


## Dealer service executives view service schedules

that are shared by the OEM and by the customers.
They procure parts and raise requests for product
transfers from other dealerships to fulfill orders for
delivery or installation. They also track warranties
and come up with periodic plans for body work,
cleaning, and maintenance.


## Financial Officer

Maximizes portfolio and vehicle lifetime value by
determining the next best action for a vehicle.


## Financial officers are responsible for submitting

proof-of-sales and retail delivery reports to the
OEM. They also help with offering car financing to
customers, extending service contracts, and
analyzing rebates and incentives offered by the
OEM.


## Service Agent

Manages communication with customers in call
centers and assist with their queries and
complaints.


## Service agents are the points of contact between

customers and service technicians. They make
informed recommendations to customers based
on the vehicle history, previous cases, and parts
requirements.


## Sales and Operations Manager

Monitors inventory and develop improvement
plans for developmental efficiency.


## Sales and operations managers find ways to

increase collaboration between dealers and
OEMs, and dealers and customers. They manage
budgets and forecasts, perform quality controls
and monitor production KPIs, and train and
supervise staff.

Appraisal Management

Use these objects to process appraisals and estimate vehicle valuations.

<!-- page:19 -->






## Appraisal

Stores the information for a vehicle appraisal requested by a customer, such as the appraisal
purpose, the validity end date, and the appraisal fee.



## Appraisal Item

Stores information about a vehicle that's appraised, such as its model year, make name, model
name, and trim type.

<!-- page:20 -->




## Appraisal Item Add On

Stores information about any modifications done and additional accessories added to a vehicle
that's appraised.



## Appraisal Item Provider Valuation

Stores information about the appraisal valuations generated by external market sources.


Captive Finance Information

Use these objects to track the financial information about your customers and partners.





## Financial Account

Stores information about different financing and investment options, such as vehicle loans or
leases.

<!-- page:21 -->




## Financial Account Party

Stores information about the role assumed by a person or organization involved with a financial
account, such as a beneficiary or a trustee.



## Party Financial Asset

Stores information about the relationship between an account or contact and an asset that’s
covered under a lease or loan.



## Financial Account Party Financial Asset

Stores information about the relationship between a party financial asset and a financial
account, such as a trade-in vehicle related to a customer’s lease, or a collateral asset related to a
customer’s loan.



## Party Financial Asset Additional Owner

Stores information about the relationship between additional or secondary people and an asset
that’s covered under a lease or loan.



## Financial Account Balance

Stores information about the total balance, principal balance, or the available credit for a loan or
lease.



## Financial Account Transaction

Stores information about each debit or credit transaction related to a loan or a lease.



## Financial Account Address

Stores information about each address related to a loan or lease, such as a communication
address, a billing address, or a statement address.



## Financial Account Statement

Stores information about each periodic or account closure statement related to a loan or a lease.



## Financial Account Fee

Stores information about different types of fees levied on a loan or lease account, such as
processing fees, late payment fees, or wear and tear fees.



## Financial Account Milestone

Stores information about key events or moments in a loan or lease account’s lifecycle, such as
vehicle title transfer, deferred payments, or delinquency.



## Asset Title

Stores information about the legal ownership of a vehicle or an asset.

<!-- page:22 -->




## Asset Title Party

Stores information about the relationship between an account, a contact, or a user and a vehicle
or asset title.


Fleet Management

Use these objects to track and monitor fleets of vehicles or other assets.

<!-- page:23 -->






## Fleet

Stores information about a group of assets or vehicles used in commercial or transport
operations.



## Fleet Asset

Stores information about the relationship between an asset or a vehicle and the fleet to which it

<!-- page:24 -->



belongs.



## Fleet Participant

Stores information about the relationship between a fleet and a participant, such as an account,
a contact, or a user.


Groups and Households

Use these objects to manage groups and households.





## Account Contact Relationship

Stores information about the relationship of a contact with multiple accounts. For example, a
members belonging to multiple households.



## Account Account Relationship

Stores information about the relationship between two business accounts. For example, a
household and its preferred dealership.



## Contact Contact Relationship

Stores information about the relationship between two contacts or two person accounts. For

<!-- page:25 -->



example, a customer and a lawyer at a law firm.



## Party Role Relationship

Stores information about the roles of two accounts or two contacts that are related to each
other. For example, the wife and husband relationship between two contacts.


Inventory Management

Use these objects to track inventory and product movement.

<!-- page:26 -->




Note To learn about the objects that you can use to store information about inventory, see Field
Service Inventory Management Data Model.


## Product Item

Stores information about the stock of a product at a particular location. A product item is
associated with an inventory location, such as a warehouse or a distribution lot, and a product.



## Product Item Transaction

Stores information about an action taken on a product item. Product item transactions are auto-
generated records that help you track when a product item is consumed, replenished, adjusted,
or transferred.



## Product Request

Stores information about an order or a request for a part or parts.



## Product Request Line Item

Stores information about each product that’s a part of a product request. Product request line
items are components of product requests.



## Product Transfer

Stores information about the transfer or the movement of inventory between two inventory
locations.



## Product Transfer State

Stores information about an action taken to associate a serialized product to a product transfer
and indicates the action done to the serialized product. The new state is a result of the action.
The product transfer state is created when serialized products are attached to the product
transfer.



## Shipment

Stores information about the transport or shipment of inventory between two inventory
locations.



## Shipment Line Item

Stores information about a product that is included in a shipment.



## Product Required

Stores information about a product that you need to complete a work order or work order line
item.



## Product Consumed

Stores information about a product that is used to complete a work order or work order line
item.

<!-- page:27 -->




## Product Consumed State

Stores information about the new state of the product after it’s consumed.



## Return Order

Stores information about the return, recall, or repair of products.



## Return Order Line Item

Stores information about a product that is returned or repaired as part of a return order.



## Return Order Item Adjustment

Stores information about a price adjustment on a return order line item.



## Serialized Product

Stores information about the serial number for a product in an inventory.



## Serialized Product Transaction

Stores information about a transaction that is performed on a serialized product.


Leads and Opportunity Management

Use these objects to access enhanced lead management features.

<!-- page:28 -->






## Lead

Stores information about prospective customers who are interested in buying, selling, or trading-
in vehicles or other parts ad accessories.



## Lead Line Item

Stores information about products related to a lead, such as vehicles, parts, or accessories.
Multiple lead line items can be related to a Lead record. Each lead line item is related to one
Product record.

Note Use the Asset Identification Number field to relate a lead line item with a specific
vehicle or part.

<!-- page:29 -->




## Lead Preferred Seller

Stores information about the preferred dealer or provider account of a lead. Multiple lead
preferred sellers can be related to one Lead record. Each lead preferred seller is related to one
Account record.



## Opportunity

Stores information about potential customers, converted from leads, who are interested in price
negotiations, test drives, and dealer interactions.



## Opportunity Product

Stores information about products related to an opportunity, such as vehicles, parts, or
accessories. Multiple opportunity products can be related to one Opportunity record. Each
opportunity product is related to one Product record.



## Opportunity Preferred Seller

Stores information about the preferred dealer or provider account of an opportunity. Multiple
opportunity preferred sellers can be related to one Opportunity record. Each opportunity
preferred seller is related to one Account record.


Partner Visit Management

Use these objects to plan and execute visits.

<!-- page:30 -->





Note You can use Action Plan Template and Action Plan for purposes other than visit management.
For example, you can create action plans on Asset Account Participant, Asset Milestone, Fleet, or
Vehicle.


## Action Plan Template

Stores information about a set of frequently performed generic visit tasks and associated
assessment indicator definitions. Each action plan template can be used to create multiple
action plans.



## Action Plan

Stores information about the list of tasks related to a specific visit, along with information on
which tasks are mandatory. Each action plan is related to a visit and an action plan template.



## Visit

Stores information about visit data, such as the account, location, time, priority, and visitor. Each
visit is related to one or more action plans and one or more generic visit tasks.

<!-- page:31 -->




## Generic Visit Task

Stores information about an audit task to be performed during a visit, such as status, time, and
sequence of the task.



## Generic Visit Task Context

Stores information about common attributes that are used for different types of contexts for the
tasks that can be performed during the visit. Generic Visit Task and Generic Visit Task Context
have a many-to-many relationship.



## Generic Visit Task Context Relation

Stores information connecting a specific task with a specific task context. This is a junction object
between Generic Visit Task and Generic Visit Task Context.



## Assessment Indicator Definition

Stores information about the parameters or metrics for each task. Multiple assessment indicator
definitions are related to a generic visit task context and a generic visit key performance
indicator.



## Generic Visit Key Performance Indicator

Stores information about the target and actual values for the assessment indicator definitions
related to a visit. Generic Visit Key Performance Indicator and Generic Visit Task Context have a
many-to-many relationship.


Retail Sales Information

Use these objects to submit sales reports for vehicles and parts.

<!-- page:32 -->






## Rebate Claim

Stores information about the vehicle that’s sold by a dealer, such as odometer reading at the
time of delivery, delivery status, processing status, and sale approval date. Rebate Claim has a
many-to-many relationship with both Asset and Vehicle records.

<!-- page:33 -->




## Transaction Journal

Stores information about the product or product category of the parts that are sold by a dealer
along with other details, such as the asset or vehicle reference number, the traded-to and traded-
from accounts, and the quantity sold. The polymorphic field Reference record can be related to
an Asset or a Vehicle record.


Stakeholder Management

Use these objects to manage stakeholders.





## Asset Account Participant

Stores information about accounts related to an asset. Each asset can have multiple related
stakeholder accounts, each with a different role.



## Asset Contact Participant

Stores information about contacts related to an asset. Each asset can have multiple related
stakeholder contacts, each with a different role.

<!-- page:34 -->




## Internal Organization Unit

Stores information about internal department structures with multiple accounts related to a
single internal organization unit.



## Business Profile

Stores detailed information about an account, such as the registered name of the partner, type
of services offered by the partner, and the associated region. Each business profile is related to
one Account record.



## Party Relationship Group

Stores information about groups of people related to an account such as a customer household,
or a dealer group that purchases vehicles and services. Multiple party relationship groups can be
related to an Account record.


Test Drive and Vehicle Service Appointments

Use these objects to plan and execute test drive and vehicle service appointments.

<!-- page:35 -->





Note Not all Salesforce Scheduler objects are shown in this data model diagram. View the
Salesforce Scheduler data model on the Salesforce Architect page to see the complete data model.


## Service Territory

Stores information about a dealer’s geographical territory. Service appointments are scheduled
for a specific service territory.



## Service Resource

Stores information about the users who can be added to service appointments, and the assets
such as vehicles used in test drive appointments.

<!-- page:36 -->




## Skill

Stores information about the expertise and competencies of service resources.



## Work Type

Stores information about the key parameters for a specific type of service provided by a dealer,
such as the estimated duration taken to perform the task, the prep and wrap-up buffer timings,
and appointment category.



## Work Type Group

Stores information about a type of service offered by a dealer such as periodic vehicle checkup,
test drive appointment, or body and paint work. Each work type group is related to a service
territory by a work type.



## Operating Hours

Stores information about the timezone and hours in which a service territory, service resource, or
work type is available for appointments.



## Time Slot

Stores information about the period of time on a specified day of the week during which
appointments can be scheduled.



## Service Resource Skill

Stores information about the relationship of a service resource and their skills. A service resource
can be related to multiple skills.



## Skill Requirement

Stores information about the relationship of a work type and the skills required to perform the
work. A work type can have multiple skill requirements.



## Work Type Group Member

Stores information about the relationship of a work type and the work type group it belongs to.



## Service Territory Member

Stores information about the relationship of a service resource with a service territory. Multiple
service resources of type Technician and Asset can be assigned to a service territory.



## Service Appointment

Stores information about a test drive or a vehicle service appointment such as the status, the
associated service territory, estimated duration, and comments.


Vehicle Information

Use these objects to manage products and assets.

<!-- page:37 -->






## Business Brand

Stores information about brands of products that help with customer engagement. Multiple
products can be related to one business brand.



## Product

Stores information about each item in the product portfolio, such as a vehicle model or a part
type. Use automotive-specific fields on Product to define parts or vehicle models.

Note To learn about other objects related to Product that you can use to store information
about inventory, see the Field Service data model. To learn about other objects related to
Product that you can use to store information about retail commerce, see the B2C
Commerce data model.



## Asset

Stores information about a built or sold physical asset instance, such as a vehicle or a part. An
asset can have multiple related child assets.



## Asset Milestone

Stores information about key events or moments in an asset’s lifecycle, such as the milestone

<!-- page:38 -->



type, milestone location, milestone date, and stage. Each asset can have multiple related asset
milestones.



## Asset Warranty

Stores information about warranties related to an asset, such as the warranty type, warranty date,
and details of parts and labor covered. Each asset can have multiple related asset warranties.



## Asset Relationships

Stores information about replacement, upgrade, or cross-grade assets related to an asset. Each
asset can have multiple asset relationships.



## Vehicle Definition

Stores information about a typical vehicle model, such as stylistic specifications, battery and
engine specifications, and fuel economy specifications.

Note The data model allows a Product record to be related to multiple Vehicle Definition
records. However, in Automotive Cloud, a Product record must be related to only one
Vehicle Definition record to ensure data consistency.



## Vehicle

Stores information about a specific vehicle instance, such as registration details, vehicle
identification number, and odometer readings. Each vehicle has one related Asset record.

Note First, create records in the Asset object and then, create Vehicle records for assets that
are vehicles. Use the Vehicle field on Asset to relate the asset with a vehicle.



Warranty Claims

Use these objects to track and adjudicate dealer claims.

<!-- page:39 -->






## Claim

Stores information about a claim to request a manufacturer to repair, replace, or refund for a
defective asset, such as its status, type, reason, and account.

<!-- page:40 -->




## Claim Item

Stores information about a defective asset related to a claim, such as its usage, fault date, and
repair date.



## Claim Coverage

Stores information about a casual part in a defective asset, such as its fault code and coverage
type.



## Claim Coverage Payment Detail

Stores information about the labor services, part replacements, or expenses claimed to rectify a
causal part.
