Add Vehicles or Assets to Fleets from the Vehicle or Asset List View Page

1.    From the App Launcher, search for and select Vehicles.
2.    Select the vehicles to add to a fleet.
3.    Click Add Vehicles To Fleet.
4.    Select whether you want to add the vehicles to a new or existing fleet.
5.    Click Next.
6.    Enter the fleet details as required.
7.    Click Next.
8.    Enter the effective dates and status for your fleet asset attributes.
9.    Click Add To Fleet.
10.    Repeat the same to add Assets to a fleet from the Asset List View page.



## Add Vehicles or Assets to Fleets from the Fleet Record Page using Criteria-

Based Search and Filter

1. From the App Launcher, search for and select Fleets.
2. Open a fleet record page.
3. On Vehicle Search, enter a search criteria.
4. From the results, select the vehicles you want to add to the fleet.
5. Click Add Vehicles To Fleet.
Make sure the selected vehicle searchable field records have an asset associated with them before
adding them to a fleet.
6. Enter the effective dates and status for your fleet asset attributes.
7. Click Add To Fleet.
8. Similarly, add assets to fleets.

## Set Up Vehicle and Asset Lending for Automotive Cloud

Set up and configure features for Vehicle and Asset Lending for a captive finance or a banking
organization. Set up product catalogs, pricing procedures, disclosures and consent, stage
management, compliant data sharing, qualification rules, and document intake. Set up additional
features that support vehicle loans and leases, right from intake to decisioning. Help underwriters,
financial managers, customers, agents, and dealers manage and track applications and proposals. The
vehicle lending processes are built on top of the Digital Lending platform that supports the end-to-end
lending lifecycle across all retail loan products and channels in a unified lending platform.
Submit and Track Vehicle Loan and Lease Applications
Submit applications for vehicle leases and loans as a customer, or as an agent on behalf of the
customer. Customers can use the Experience Cloud site to submit applications using a self-service
guided flow, and track the submitted applications. Agents can use the Agent Assisted Application
Management app to submit applications on behalf of clients using the same guided flow.
Assess and Analyze Vehicle Loans and Leases
Use the Vehicle & Asset Lending app in Automitive Cloud as an underwriter to assess and analyze the
applications submitted by customers or by agents on behalf of customers. Underwriters can configure
stipulations, gather insights about the applicant, analyze the applicant profile and credit history, and
create proposals. After negotiations and review, the underwriters can share the final proposals with
dealers for acceptance or rejection.
Review and Close Vehicle Loan and Lease Proposals
Dealers can use the Experience Cloud site to review and track vehicle loan and lease applications after
the intake process, modify and negotiate terms during the decisioning process, and finally accept or
reject proposals after the underwriter shares the final proposal with them. Dealers can efficiently
rehash applications by either modifying the vehicle or the financial terms to come up with better loan
or lease proposals. Dealers can use the Work Guide on an Application Form Product record page on
the Experience Cloud site to then accept or reject the final proposal with just one click.

Configure fees, interest rates, and repayment terms for loans and leases. Set up a pricing procedure to

Disclosures and Consents for Vehicle and Asset Lending
When customers and agents apply for a vehicle loan or lease, they consent to the disclosures related
to governmental or agency-based regulations and policies. Set up disclosure text and consent
authorization forms for different types of lending processes in your company.

Stage Management for Vehicle and Asset Lending
Create a transition plan for each stage of the vehicle and asset lending application process. Set up
stage management to restrict the values of the Stage field on an Application Form Product so that only

Help dealers, agents, and customers efficiently rehash loan or lease proposals for Vehicle and Asset
Lending. Set up the product configurator, Omnistudio components, and other features for a seamless

a loan or lease and come up with better offers. Set up Criteria-Based Search and Filter to change the
vehicle selected in a proposal.
Set Up Funding Workbench
Set up components for enabling and accessing the capabilities of Funding Workbench.
Configure the Review Offer Flow in Vehicle and Asset Lending
Create a flow from a predefined template that triggers an action item to share an offer for review with
an application form product participant such as the customer, dealer, or underwriter. When an
underwriter selects a proposal as final, they can use the screen flow embedded in the underwriter’s
console to quickly assign an action item to a participant. The participant can be a dealer, another
underwriter, or the customer who applied. The participant is notified by email when the proposal is
shared by them and they can analyze it further and take a decision on the offer.
Configure Countries and States for Vehicle and Asset Lending
Configure the picklist values for countries in the Omniscript-based guided intake flow to provide a
customer's address for a loan or lease application.
Make Dealers Available for Selection in Vehicle and Asset Lending
When customers apply for vehicle loans or leases, or when agents apply on behalf of customers, they
select a dealership that they prefer based on the dealership name, city, or postal code. Create dealers
in your org to make them available for selection as preferred dealerships during the application intake
process.
Qualification Rules for Vehicle and Asset Lending
Use Product Catalog Management, Context Service, and Business Rules Engine to create qualification
rules that drive eligibility of products for applicants. You can create qualification rules to determine
which financial products are displayed to applicants based on criteria such as age, region, service level
agreements, or tier. You can ensure that applicants don't select conflicting products, or they select
multiple products that are offered in combination.
Know Your Customer for Vehicle and Asset Lending

## Configure Product Attributes for Vehicle and Asset Lending

Create attributes for your financial products to specify the maximum and minimum amount for a loan
or lease, the validity of the offer, and the duration of a loan or lease. When customers and agents
apply for a loan or lease, these attributes are displayed as options for selection and are also used to
calculate the offers. Additionally, create a product attribute to specify the Omniscript name that’s used
by the application to launch a guided flow during the application intake process for agents and
customers.
Create Attribute Picklists for Vehicle and Asset Lending
Create an attribute picklist and its related values for product attributes of data type Picklist. For
example, loan term or lease term is a picklist-type attribute with possible values of 12 months, 24
months, or 48 months. The attribute values you specify are displayed as options to a customer or an
agent and also used to calculate the offers during the application intake process.
Create Product Classifications for Vehicle and Asset Lending

Captive finance lenders rely on credit reports and scores from major bureaus such as Experian, Equifax,
TransUnion, and CIBIL to make informed lending decisions. These reports provide a comprehensive
overview of a borrower’s credit history, including their payment behavior, outstanding debts, and overall
financial responsibility. By analyzing this data, lenders can assess the likelihood of loan repayment, with
higher credit scores typically leading to more favorable terms such as lower interest rates. Lenders can
offer better financing options to borrowers with good credit, cross-check information for identity

<!-- page:610 -->



verification, and detect fraud or past delinquencies, ensuring the accuracy of a borrower's financial
information.

Here's how Automotive Cloud integrates the credit bureau data into automated underwriting systems to
streamline the loan approval process and ensure that lenders comply with fair lending regulations by
conducting proper due diligence.
