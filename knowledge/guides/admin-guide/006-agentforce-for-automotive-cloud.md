Agentforce for Automotive Cloud

Agentforce for Automotive Cloud provides an easy way to manage customer account information, sales
and service requirements for vehicles, assets, dealers, and service process requests for Automotive loans
or leases through natural language interactions. With out-of-the-box agent subagents and actions,
efficiently generate performance summaries, create cases and tasks, and update records in a systematic
flow.


**REQUIRED EDITIONS**


Available in: Lightning Experience

Available in: Enterprise, Performance, Unlimited, and Developer Editions with the Agentforce for
Automotive add-on or included in Agentforce 1 Automotive Edition. Requires each user to have the
Agentforce for Automotive add-on to access the action.



## Agent Templates in Automotive Cloud

Use the agent templates in Automotive cloud to efficiently run your automotive business by using
Agentforce.
Agentforce for Asset Finance Management
Streamline your asset finance operations, such as service processes related to modifying the payment
details of loans or leases. Customer support reps can help users defer payments, modify payment due

dates, reverse fees, or view transactions and balances. This agent facilitates related actions such as

Agentforce for Asset Service Management
Streamline vehicle and asset services. Service reps in an original equipment manufacturing company
can efficiently summarize asset telemetry, create service quotes and work orders based on the
information selected by a user, including relevant coverages for products such as asset warranties,
service contracts, or entitlements as well as manual discounts. This agent facilitates related actions
such as drafting emails.
Agentforce for Collections and Recovery Assistance
Boost your collections teams' efficiency with Collections and Recovery Assistance, a personal AI Agent
integrated into the Collections and Recovery feature. Create an agent using the Collections and
Recovery Assistance template.
Agentforce for Channel Revenue Management
Simplify the management of rebate programs and payout processes through natural language
interactions. With predefined subagents and actions, track calculations, resolve issues with payout
insights, and review overall rebate program details to ensure transparency, traceability, and accurate
claims.
Agentforce for Industries Customer Engagement
Streamline the lifecycle of vehicles and related operations. Sales and service reps in an automotive
company can efficiently manage customer accounts, vehicles, financial information of an account, and
search for location-based dealerships on behalf of customers. This agent facilitates related actions
such as creating tasks, capturing opportunities, and scheduling test drives or service appointments.
Agentforce for Industries Sales Assistance
Streamline the sales life-cycle of products, such as vehicles or assets, by supporting more AI-assisted
task automation and increasing productivity of sales reps. This agent helps to generate record
summaries such as that of a selected vehicle or asset, search for dealers, search for products and
relevant accessories, search and facilitate actions such as creating opportunities and quotes, initiating
appraisals, and drafting emails in a single flow.
Agentforce for Inventory Management
Helps users efficiently monitor and manage inventory using natural language queries. It supports
actions like finding products and vehicles, summarizing stock levels, and creating product requests. By
mapping user input to relevant search criteria, the agent delivers accurate, contextual results. Whether
it's about locating a specific product or part, checking stock levels, or an inventory manager creating a
replenishment product request, the agent streamlines end-to-end inventory interactions for faster
decision-making.
Agentforce for Sales Planning
Helps your sales team plan more effectively and operate efficiently. The Sales Planning agent can
answer questions about your sales agreements, generate performance summaries, and assist with
related actions such as setting up visits or drafting emails. The agent can also perform bulk updates on
sales agreements, and update records based on related data. In Program Based Business, use the
agent to quickly convert the manufacturing program component forecast fact records to
opportunities.

## Subagent: Automotive Payment Deferral

Customer support reps, account managers, and financial advisors can use the Automotive Payment

<!-- page:272 -->



Deferral subagent to defer recurring loan or lease payments on behalf of customers. Agentforce helps
to capture the new terms for the loan or lease payments and check a customer’s eligibility for the

customer gives their consent.
Subagent: Automotive Payment Due Date Modification
Customer support reps, account managers, and financial advisors can use the Automotive Payment
Due Date Modification subagent to modify the due date for recurring loan or lease payments on
behalf of customers. Agentforce helps to capture the new terms for the loan or lease payments and
check a customer’s eligibility for the due date modification request. If eligible, a case is created for the
request based on the new terms after the customer gives their consent.
Subagent: Transfer Funds to Own Account
The Funds Transfer to Own Account subagent retrieves all financial accounts associated with the
customer, checks for any account restraints, and prompts the user to select the transfer type. The
subagent then gathers the required details and creates a case for funds transfer.
Subagent: Address Update
The Address Update subagent retrieves all addresses associated with the customer, prompts the user
to select one, gathers the new address details, and creates a case for the update.
Subagent: Fee Reversal
The Fee Reversal subagent collects financial account details, displays all relevant fee transactions, and
prompts the user to select the specific transaction to create a case for reversal.
Subagent: Request Loan Payoff Statement
The Request Loan Payoff Statement subagent collects details such as the selected loan account, future
payoff date, and delivery method. Confirms all details and creates a case to fulfill the request.
Subagent: Financial Account Balances
The Financial Account Balances subagent retrieves the financial account balances associated with a
specific financial account.
Subagent: Financial Account Transactions
The Financial Account Transactions subagent retrieves all financial account transactions associated
with a specific financial account.
Subagent: Financial Account Statement
The Financial Account Statement subagent requests to order copies of customers' loan or lease
account statements.
