Authoring vs Runtime: Flows, journeys, and scenarios are authoring-layer constructs (human organization of test intent). Test cases and chains are runtime-layer (executor composition). This template populates the authoring layer only — runtime composition happens later via the pipeline.

Mandatory record-type splits: Product (Vehicle / Parts) at Foundation step 11; Asset (Vehicle / Parts) at next-tier step 13. Connected org autodemoNag is READ-ONLY — no inserts/updates/deletes/deploys.
