# Configure Vehicles & Assets

Interactive wizard for the Vehicles, Assets & Fleets journey.

## Steps

1. Confirm Asset record types **Vehicle** and **Parts** exist with separate page layouts.
2. Per-RT field setup (see `auto-vehicle-asset-fleet` skill).
3. Walk through Vehicle records linking to Vehicle Definition.
4. Configure Asset Account Participant + Asset Contact Participant for multi-stakeholder modeling.
5. (If captive finance / lending in scope) configure Asset Title + Asset Title Party.
6. Verify Asset OWD (Internal Public Read/Write, External Private). Vehicle inherits Asset's sharing.
