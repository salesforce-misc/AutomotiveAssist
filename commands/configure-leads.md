# Configure Leads & Opportunities

Interactive wizard for Partner Lead Management.

## Steps

1. Verify `Partner Lead Management` PSL assignments.
2. Setup → Partner Lead Management → Enable Partner Lead Management.
3. **Enable Partner Lead Management Default Mappings** (preserves Lead Line Item / Preferred Seller through conversion).
4. Customize Lead page layout (see `auto-leads-opportunities` skill):
   - Add Earliest / Latest Interest Date fields.
   - Add Products, Preferred Sellers, Service Appointments related lists.
   - Add **Schedule Test Drive** button (Standard Buttons section).
   - **Remove default Schedule Appointment quick action**.
   - Add Related Lists + Interest Tags components.
5. Repeat for Opportunity layout.
6. (Experience Cloud) verify `Partner Lead Management for Experience Cloud` PSL.
