# Configure Finance Console

Interactive wizard for captive finance and lending.

## Steps

1. **Confirm Timeline is enabled first** — irreversible org-wide toggle. Required before Vehicle and Asset Finance Additional Components.
2. PSL: `Vehicle and Asset Finance` (internal), `Vehicle and Asset Finance Foundation for Experience Cloud` (external).
3. Setup → Vehicle and Asset Finance Settings → Enable Vehicle and Asset Finance.
4. Enable Vehicle and Asset Finance Additional Components.
5. Configure Financial Account model (Account, Party, Balance, Transaction, Fee, Milestone, Statement, Address).
6. Configure Party Financial Asset model.
7. Configure Asset Title + Asset Title Party.
8. (For lending) PSLs: `Vehicle and Asset Lending`, `Vehicle and Asset Lending for Agents`, `Stage Management Design User`.
9. Configure Compliant Data Sharing — Stage Definitions + Stage Transitions.
10. Integrate Experian + Equifax via MuleSoft.
